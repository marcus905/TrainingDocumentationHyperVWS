## Final perspective

The objective is not to memorize Hyper-V commands.

Students should leave able to:

~~~text
Understand
 -> Build
 -> Validate
 -> Measure
 -> Break safely
 -> Troubleshoot
 -> Recover
 -> Operate consistently
~~~
